About

I'm Haoran Zhao, an M.Sc. student in Computer Control and Automation at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Lihua Xie. In summer 2026, I visited Tongji University for a research exchange hosted by Prof. Kun Cao.

My research focuses on aerial robotics, especially aerial manipulation and physical interaction between UAVs and their environment. I received my B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering from Soochow University.
